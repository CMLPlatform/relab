---
title: The 9R framework
description: The ten circularity strategies, R0 to R9, that frame what Relab records during disassembly.
---

Relab is built around the 9R framework: ten circularity strategies, R0 to R9,
ordered from smartest to last resort. The higher a product stays on the
ladder, the more of its value survives.

## Smarter product use and manufacture

| #   | Strategy | Definition                                                                                                          |
| --- | -------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| R0  | Refuse   | Make product redundant by abandoning its function or offering the same function with a radically different product. |
| R1  | Rethink  | Make product use more intensive (e.g. sharing, or multi-functional products).                                       |
| R2  | Reduce   | Increase efficiency in manufacture or use by consuming fewer resources and materials.                               |

## Extend lifespan of product and its parts

| #   | Strategy      | Definition                                                                                                      |
| --- | ------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| R3  | Re-use        | Re-use by another consumer of a discarded product still in good condition and fulfilling its original function. |
| R4  | Repair        | Repair and maintenance of a defective product so it can be used with its original function.                     |
| R5  | Refurbish     | Restore an old product and bring it up to date.                                                                 |
| R6  | Remanufacture | Use parts of a discarded product in a new product with the same function.                                       |
| R7  | Repurpose     | Use a discarded product or its parts in a new product with a different function.                                |

## Useful application of materials

| #   | Strategy | Definition                                                                      |
| --- | -------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| R8  | Recycle  | Process materials to obtain the same (high-grade) or lower (low-grade) quality. |
| R9  | Recover  | Incineration of materials with energy recovery.                                 |

## Source

The 9R framework, after Potting et al. (2017),
[*Circular Economy: Measuring Innovation in the Product Chain*](https://www.pbl.nl/uploads/default/downloads/pbl-2016-circular-economy-measuring-innovation-in-product-chains-2544.pdf)
